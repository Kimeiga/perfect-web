E6 follow-up — cache-key auditing in `pw explain` (charter §14 M6 task 9)

  Advice, not a diagnostic. The compiler cannot tell a deliberate
  omission from a mistake — two readers sharing an entry is sometimes
  exactly what a cache is for — so it says what the key separates, what
  a dependency separates that the key does not, and lets a reader decide.

  The build generation is absent from the application section on
  purpose: PW5102 asked the author for it and was retired, because
  injecting a compatibility generation is platform mechanism.
