================= WHAT MAY *NOT* BE SAID =======================

  1. NOT "the analyses are general". 29/29 means each survived a
     program its fixture did not anticipate. That is the strongest
     thing a finite suite can say and it is not generality.

  2. NOT "the compiler cannot crash". Nine generator families found
     no panic. A coverage-guided fuzzer has never been run, and the
     receipt for why that matters is in this repository: reverting
     the arity fix left corpus-mutation and byte-soup green while
     only the generator aimed at that seam went red.

  3. NOT "the language is sound". Nothing here is a proof.

  4. NOT "no compiler knowledge is hardcoded". Four frame-phase
     keywords, the phase permission matrix, and one module name for
     `on:` attributes.

  5. NOT "the corpus was met without touching the corpus". Ten
     fixtures changed; docs/CORPUS.md has a row for each and
     corpus_history.rs runs their old text.

  6. NOT "resumption is checked". Two of its three questions are —
     serializability and privacy. The third, whether a value can be
     resumed under THIS CODE VERSION, is not addressed at all.
     PW3011 is its reserved code and the one remaining KNOWN_GAP.

  7. NOT "P0 is publishable". Corpus conformance and generality are
     two of P0's claims. Several others rest on Marko and Koka
     behind adapters, and several are unstarted. The ledger governs.
