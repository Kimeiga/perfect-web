WHAT THIS IS NOT
  Not a claim that pw and Koka agree on everything. ADR-0011 records
  four places they deliberately do not, and tests/differential_vs_koka.rs
  asserts each. This is the other direction: where the subsets overlap,
  they must not disagree.
  Not a semantic equivalence proof. Koka accepting a module says its
  types and exhaustiveness hold, not that it computes the same values.
  spikes/pw-to-koka executes one program and compares values; this
  compiles many and compares verdicts.
